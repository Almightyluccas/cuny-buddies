## Mockups

Palettes were generated in [Coolors](https://coolors.co). Each palette has the same two onboarding screens:

- **Welcome / sign in:** CUNY email login, "Find classmates who are free when you are."
- **Add your schedule:** upload a CUNYfirst schedule PDF or photo, then confirm the detected classes.
